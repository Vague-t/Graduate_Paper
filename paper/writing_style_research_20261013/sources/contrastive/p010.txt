(2) To address the challenge of how to effectively integrate temporal history and textual
semantic knowledge in Knowledge Graphs, this thesis proposes the Semantic-augmented
Contrastive Historical Modeling Model with Prefix-tuning for Temporal Knowledge Graph
Reasoning (ChapTER). ChapTER uses a two-tower architecture based on pre-trained
language models, and it converts the temporal knowledge reasoning task into a
query-candidate matching task. As for the input, ChapTER constructs the input prompt with
historical contexts, and it is trained in a self-supervised contrastive manner. By applying the
prefix-tuning method with virtual time prefix, ChapTER can effectively integrate temporal
history and textual semantics in Temporal Knowledge Graphs, and perform temporal
knowledge reasoning in different scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that ChapTER
achieves superior performance compared to competitive baselines in both transductive and
few-shot inductive temporal knowledge reasoning tasks with only 0.17% parameters tuned.
In addition, the model's training time consumption was reduced by 21.4%, demonstrating the
effectiveness and efficiency of ChapTER to perform temporal knowledge reasoning in
various complex scenarios.
In summary, the thesis conducts in-depth research in the field of knowledge reasoning,
focusing on the key issue of capturing and integrating heterogeneous information in
Knowledge Graphs. It proposes a schema-augmented multi-level contrastive learning model
to capture and integrate the structural features and contextual semantics in Knowledge
Graphs. Besides, it designs a semantic-augmented contrastive historical modeling model to
capture and integrate the temporal history and textual semantics in Knowledge Graphs,
enhancing the model's ability to capture and integrate heterogeneous information in complex
knowledge reasoning scenarios.
