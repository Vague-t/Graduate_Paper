Abstract
With the advancement of text generation technology, automated machine writing has
deeply integrated into people’s lives, and the generation of texts with personalized styles
according to user requirements has become an urgent need. Against this backdrop, the task of
text style transfer has emerged, aiming to accurately transfer the text from the original style to
the target style while retaining the content of the input text and ensuring language fluency. In
past research on text style transfer, researchers often focused only on simple English text style
transfer tasks such as emotional transfer, characterized by a low coupling degree between text
style and content. However, users’ style transfer needs are becoming increasingly complex, and
complex text styles are often highly coupled with content, making existing methods unable to
meet practical needs. Moreover, with the growing global influence of Chinese users and the
emphasis on inheriting Chinese culture, Classical Chinese has received increasing attention.
However, there are few models capable of understanding and generating both Modern and
Classical Chinese at this stage. Therefore, this thesis focuses on the text style transfer task
between Modern and Classical Chinese and explores this area further.
Currently, the research on text style transfer between Modern and Classical Chinese faces
the following two challenges: (1) The text style is highly coupled with content, and the style
attributes are complex. With limited computational resources and a lack of annotated data, the
style extraction and generation capabilities of existing small models cannot meet the task
requirements; (2) With sufficient computational resources, although large language models
have stronger understanding and generation capabilities, they have not yet aligned well with
human preferences in the field of text style transfer, and the illusions of large models can also
interfere with content information.

models with limited computational resources, a text style transfer algorithm based on prompt-
enhanced reinforcement learning is proposed, which completes the style transfer between
Modern and Classical Chinese without sacrificing the integrity of content information and the
fluency of generated language. Experimental results show that this method can effectively
understand and transfer the complex styles of Modern and Classical Chinese. Compared to the
