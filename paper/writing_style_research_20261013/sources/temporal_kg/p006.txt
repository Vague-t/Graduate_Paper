基于结构增强语言模型的时序知识图谱推理方法研究
ABSTRACT
TemporalKnowledgeGraphs(TKGs)areaclassofstructuredknowledgerepresentations
that capture the temporal evolution of relationships among entities, and have demonstrated
significant value in applications such as question answering, recommendation systems, and
financial analysis. Compared with traditional static knowledge graphs, TKGs not only re-
quire modeling structural dependencies among entities and relations, but also capturing their
dynamic temporal characteristics, thereby providing essential support for reasoning in dy-
namicenvironments. However,duetotheinherentsparsityandincompletenessofreal-world
data,TKGsstillcontainalargenumberofunobservedfactsandevents. Therefore,designing
effective temporal knowledge graph reasoning (TKGR) methods for accurate prediction of
latentknowledge remainsacriticalresearchchallenge.
With the advancement of deep learning, recent studies have explored integrating multi-
sourceheterogeneousinformationinTKGs(e.g.,structuralinformationandtextualsemantics)
with temporal modeling to enhance reasoning capabilities. In particular, language models
have shown strong advantages in semantic understanding and representation, offering a new
paradigm for TKGR. Nevertheless, existing language model-based approaches still face two
key challenges. First, language models rely on sequence modeling, which differs fundamen-
tally from the structured representation of TKGs, making it difficult to effectively capture
complex structural dependencies and global temporal evolution patterns. Second, most ex-
isting methods lack interpretability, as they rely on implicit representation learning without
explicitly modeling the reasoning process, resulting in limited transparency and reliability in
practicalapplications.
To address these challenges, this thesis conducts a systematic study on temporal knowl-
edge graph reasoning based on structure-enhanced language models. The objective is to de-
velop a unified framework that integrates structural and semantic information through joint
modeling,therebyenhancingthecapabilityoflanguagemodelstocapturecomplexstructural
dependencies and temporal evolution patterns, while improving prediction accuracy and in-
terpretability. Themaincontributions are summarized asfollows:
