Abstract
In recent years, there has been a rapid development of deep learning techniques,
resulting in revolutionary breakthroughs in various fields. Within the field of deep learning,
the Transformer model based on attention mechanisms has proven to be a highly effective
modeling framework. This has led to the development of many representative models, with
ChatGPT in particular sparking a new wave of AI revolution. The effectiveness of the
Transformer model is largely due to the attention mechanism's ability to capture global

other dimensions of the overall model structure. As a result, there is still room for
improvement in both model effectiveness and efficiency.
This thesis aims to optimize the Transformer model structure and proposes a
comprehensive optimization and acceleration scheme, CSformer, based on intra-layer
clustering and inter-layer selection. The scheme includes the following main components: 1)
intra-layer clustering and clustering of sequences within a single layer of the stacked encoder
or decoder of the Transformer model, which replaces complete attention calculation with
content-based sparse attention. To improve the accuracy of clustering, several methods are
proposed for cluster center vector generation, cluster center information enhancement, and
cluster loss calculation at different stages of clustering. 2) A hierarchical fusion method for
classification information is proposed to optimize the performance of the clustering attention
model in classification tasks. 3) For measuring the contribution of tokens, three token
