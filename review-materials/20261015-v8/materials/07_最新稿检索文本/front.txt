[V8-P0034] 摘要

[V8-P0035] 论辩挖掘旨在识别文本中的论点、前提及其支持与攻击关系，为写作评价、舆情分析等应用提供结构化依据。大语言模型能够从原始文本直接生成论辩结构，但组件与关系之间的相互依赖仍给模型学习和结果修正带来困难：局部改写可能改变组件的论辩角色或关联关系，使继承的标注成为错误监督；预测图中的组件错误会影响多条关系，独立修正局部对象又难以兼顾整体结构。

[V8-P0036] 针对上述问题，论文分别研究增强监督的可靠利用与预测结构的协调修正：前者改善模型学习所依据的监督信号，后者在不更新抽取模型参数的条件下，联合决定组件及其关联关系的修改。具体研究内容如下：

[V8-P0037] （1）针对局部改写后原标注可能失效的问题，提出可靠监督与一致性学习方法。该方法先确定改写可能影响的组件与关系范围，并建立原文与改写文本之间的组件对应关系，再通过语义审核筛选仍然可靠的组件类型和关系状态。训练时，原文提供完整的论辩图监督，改写文本仅在通过审核的部分提供辅助监督与一致性约束，从而利用增强数据补充学习信号并减少错误监督的影响。在英文议论文与公共政策评论两个语料上的实验表明，与同骨干的基准模型相比，该方法使严格关系F1分别由65.69%和41.79%提高至68.25%和45.08%。

[V8-P0038] （2）针对组件错误及其关联关系难以协调修正的问题，提出结构约束联合修正方法。该方法将组件修改与全部受影响关系的重建组织为共同接受或拒绝的操作，在固定候选与对象评分的条件下，结合结构约束、分项效用和编辑预算选择修正结果，以控制修改范围并减少对原有正确结构的破坏。对照实验表明，联合修正直接作用于基准模型时能够改善抽取性能，在第一项研究得到的模型上仍可继续修正残余错误，使两个语料的严格关系F1进一步提高至71.63%和50.77%；与相同预算的重新生成相比，联合修正取得了更高的F1，并保留了98%以上的原有正确关系。

[V8-P0039] 在同一开源大语言模型系列的三种骨干配置上，两项方法均取得性能提升。对照结果表明，可靠监督与一致性学习能够独立改善抽取效果，结构约束联合修正能够适用于不同训练配置的预测结果，并在可靠监督学习的基础上进一步提高完整论辩图的抽取准确性。

[V8-P0040] 关键词：论辩挖掘；大语言模型；一致性学习；结构化预测；联合修正

[V8-P0041] Abstract

[V8-P0042] Argument mining identifies claims, premises, and their support or attack relations to provide structured evidence for applications such as writing assessment and public opinion analysis. Large language models can generate argument structures directly from text, but the interdependence of components and relations presents challenges for learning and repair. Local rewriting may change argumentative roles or relations, making inherited annotations unreliable. Component errors can affect multiple relations, while independent local corrections may fail to maintain structural consistency.

[V8-P0043] This thesis investigates the reliable use of augmented supervision and the coordinated repair of predicted structures. The first study improves the supervision used for learning, while the second makes joint decisions on component edits and associated relations without updating the extraction model. The main contributions are as follows.

[V8-P0044] (1) A reliable supervision and consistency learning method is proposed to address annotation invalidation after local rewriting. It identifies potentially affected components and relations, aligns components between the original and rewritten texts, and audits the validity of component types and relation states. The original text provides complete graph supervision, whereas the rewritten text contributes auxiliary supervision and consistency constraints only for accepted annotations. This uses augmented learning signals while reducing erroneous supervision. On persuasive essay and public policy comment corpora, strict relation F1 increases from 65.69% and 41.79% to 68.25% and 45.08%, respectively, compared with the baseline using the same backbone.

[V8-P0045] (2) A structure-constrained joint repair method is proposed to coordinate component corrections with their affected relations. Component edits and reconstruction of all affected relations are accepted or rejected together. With fixed candidates and object scores, structural constraints, separate utility requirements, and edit budgets guide repair decisions to limit changes and preserve correct structures. Controlled comparisons show that joint repair improves extraction when applied directly to the baseline and also corrects residual errors in the model obtained in the first study, further increasing strict relation F1 to 71.63% and 50.77% on the two corpora. Compared with regeneration under the same budget, joint repair achieves higher F1 while preserving more than 98% of previously correct relations.

[V8-P0046] Both methods improve performance across three backbone configurations from the same open-source large language model family. Controlled comparisons show that reliable supervision and consistency learning improve extraction independently, while structure-constrained joint repair can operate on predictions from different training configurations and further improve complete argument graph extraction when combined with reliable supervision learning.

[V8-P0047] Keywords: Argument Mining, Large Language Models, Consistency Learning, Structured Prediction, Joint Repair
